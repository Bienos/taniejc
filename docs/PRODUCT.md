# Product brief

**Taniej** helps people in Poland search for a product, make a reusable list and compare observed prices with clear source and age.

## Implemented journeys

1. Search by Polish phrase and view Open Food Facts catalog matches.
2. Inspect any recent Open Prices observation returned for the product barcode.
3. Add a match to a locally saved list, adjust quantity, and calculate the lowest known combination within a selected store-count limit.
4. Dictate text where browser Web Speech Recognition supports Polish.
5. Save products locally for later.
6. Scan or enter a barcode, review Open Food Facts metadata, and attach a verified product to a list item or receipt line.
7. Capture a receipt with local Polish OCR or manual entry, correct field values, classify product/discount/deposit/fee/payment lines, review deterministic shopping-list suggestions, then explicitly confirm/skip each proposed match.
8. Compare the confirmed matched subtotal with the available planned subtotal, and save confirmed exact catalog identities as device-local personal prices.
9. Review unit prices and dates from private receipt history, delete individual entries or clear local purchase history, and check the latest eligible Open Prices observation on request. A local target alert is evaluated only during that lookup.

## Data policy

A catalog match is not a price. A community price report is not guaranteed current stock or retailer-confirmed availability. No result is a complete nationwide price comparison. Missing price data stays missing. Promotions are not populated until an authorized feed exists.

Receipt images and OCR text are neither persisted nor transmitted. Corrected receipts, user-confirmed matches and resulting personal prices stay in versioned browser local storage. The app never submits receipt prices to Open Prices. OCR and match suggestions are not treated as confirmed facts until the user reviews them.

## Intended audience and outcome

Polish grocery shoppers who need a quick answer about products and a short shopping list. The first release prioritizes a truthful, low-friction workflow over national coverage claims.
